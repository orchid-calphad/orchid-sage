# In memory of Larry Kaufman

In a sense, ORCHID as a whole is an attempt to capture Larry Kaufman's CALPHAD brain, and ORCHID-SAGE, the part of the project that collects the rules and judgement of CALPHAD modellers, is dedicated to his memory.

I worked closely with Larry. He knew what almost every binary phase diagram looked like, but he also had a sense of the energy scales needed to reproduce them. He could produce very good-looking phase diagrams while doing all of the model selection and the parameter initialization in his head. That combination of knowledge of the phase diagrams, intuition for the energetics and judgement in building the models is what we are trying to make explicit, so that it can be checked, shared and used by people and by the agents that now help them.

Larry was my mentor at MIT, where I worked with him on the Zr–O and Cu–Ti–Zr systems. He was, more than anyone, the sage of CALPHAD.

— Raymundo Arróyave

*References:* L. Kaufman and H. Bernstein, *Computer Calculation of Phase Diagrams with Special Reference to Refractory Metals*, Academic Press (1970); "Obituary for Lawrence ('Larry') Kaufman", *Calphad* 45 (2014) 254–255, doi:10.1016/j.calphad.2014.02.006.
